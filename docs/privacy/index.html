AtLunch Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

AtLunch is a restaurant discovery app operated by Darryl Mak. For privacy questions or data requests, email darrylmak4@gmail.com.

Location, searches, and maps

When you grant location permission, AtLunch uses your device location to find nearby restaurants, prioritize search results, and center the map. AtLunch sends coordinates and any search text you enter through its server to Google Places. It also sends place and photo identifiers to request restaurant details and photo information. Your device loads restaurant photos and place icons directly from Google-provided URLs, which exposes your IP address to the service serving those images. The Google Maps SDK can collect IP addresses, device and SDK information, a pseudonymous SDK identifier, crash diagnostics, and map interactions. You can manage location access in Android settings.

AI restaurant summaries

To generate a summary and lunch suggestions, AtLunch sends available restaurant details through its server to OpenAI. These details can include the restaurant’s name, rating, rating count, address, phone number, and opening hours. AtLunch has disabled sharing of its API inputs and outputs for model training. Under OpenAI’s standard API settings, response data is stored for at least 30 days, and abuse-monitoring logs are generally retained for up to 30 days.

Saved restaurants and backups

AtLunch stores favorites and cached nearby results on your device. You can remove favorites in the app or clear local app data in Android settings. Android may back up and restore eligible app data depending on your device settings.

Server logs, retention, and deletion

AtLunch connects to its server over HTTPS. The server runs on Google Cloud, where request logs can include your IP address and the app’s user agent. Access to these logs in the AtLunch project is limited to the operator. Request logs expire automatically after 30 days. To ask about your data or submit a deletion request, email darrylmak4@gmail.com. AtLunch relies on automatic expiration for individual request log entries.
